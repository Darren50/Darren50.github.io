Hello! I'm Darren, a Year 2 Computer Engineering undergraduate at the National University of Singapore, on track for
Honours (Highest Distinction). I enjoy complex problem-solving, working in teams, and constantly getting better at what
I do.

Before NUS, I graduated from Singapore Polytechnic with a Diploma in Engineering with Business (with Merit) on a DSTA
Polytechnic Engineering Scholarship. During my internship at DSTA, I designed and shipped an organisation-wide 3D
modelling platform end to end as a full-stack developer.

### Software Engineer Intern, DSTA <span class="when">Mar 2022 – Aug 2022</span>
