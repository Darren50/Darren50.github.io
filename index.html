About Me

Hello! I'm Darren, a Year 2 Computer Engineering undergraduate at the National University of Singapore, on track for Honours (Highest Distinction). I'm passionate about STEM and have a zest for complex problem-solving. I enjoy working in teams to accomplish tasks and constantly strive for improvement.

Before NUS, I graduated from Singapore Polytechnic with a Diploma in Engineering with Business (with Merit) on a DSTA Polytechnic Engineering Scholarship. During my internship at DSTA, I designed and shipped an organisation-wide 3D modelling platform end to end as a full-stack developer.

These days I'm building Locked-In, an AI-assisted physical study companion robot, as part of the NUS Orbital programme. In between, you'll find me racking my brain over LeetCode problems.
